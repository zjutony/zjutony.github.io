Hi, I am Tony. 👋

I am currently a master's student in Mechanical Engineering at Zhejiang University . I received my B.Eng. degree from Zhejiang University in 2024, together with an honors degree from Chu Kochen Honors College. During my undergraduate studies, I also participated in the Intensive Training Program of Innovation and Entrepreneurship (ITP) .

My research interests include Robot-assisted Micromanipulation, Robot Learning, and AI Agent Systems. I enjoy building prototypes that bring together perception, control, and learning, and exploring how these ideas can be applied to real-world problems.

We are born too late to explore the earth and too early to explore the stars, but we are born just in time to solve robotics.
